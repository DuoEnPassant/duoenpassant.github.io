Individual teaching

A thoughtful approach to your playing.

Whether you are beginning your violin journey, returning after a break or developing more advanced repertoire, individual lessons offer time and space to focus on your own musical goals.

Lessons are adapted to your experience, interests and current challenges, combining careful technical work with musical expression.

Online lessons make it possible to study from your own home, wherever you are based, without having to travel.

What we can work on

Technique, interpretation and confidence.

01

Violin Technique

Intonation, sound production, bow control, left-hand coordination and technical challenges tailored to your playing.

02

Musical Interpretation

Repertoire, phrasing, style, musical expression and ways to develop your own artistic voice.

03

Preparation & Practice

Effective practice strategies and support with performance, audition or examination repertoire.

Your teacher

Meet Merily Leotoots.

Estonian violinist

Merily Leotoots is an Estonian violinist active in chamber music and performance. Her musical studies include the Estonian Academy of Music and Theatre, London's Guildhall School of Music & Drama and the Music and Arts University of the City of Vienna.

As a performer with Duo EnPassant and Trio Callistó, she brings an active musician's perspective to each lesson.

Study from anywhere

Flexible online learning.

Online violin lessons are available internationally in English and Estonian. All you need is your violin, a stable internet connection and a camera-equipped phone, tablet or computer.

Lesson times can be arranged individually, taking different time zones into account. In-person tuition in Tallinn is also available by arrangement.

Questions

Before your first lesson.

Can I take lessons if I live outside Estonia?

Yes. Online violin lessons are available internationally, subject to finding a mutually suitable lesson time.

What level do I need to be?

Lessons can be adapted to your experience and goals. Tell me about your current playing level when you get in touch.

What should I prepare?

Have your violin ready and briefly describe the repertoire or technical areas you would like to work on. A stable internet connection and camera are helpful.

Can I take lessons in Tallinn?

Yes. In-person lessons in Tallinn can be discussed individually.

Let's talk about your violin playing.

Introduce yourself, your experience with the violin, where you are based and what you would like to work on. We can find a suitable lesson format and time.

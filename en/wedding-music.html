Your wedding soundtrack

Two instruments. A personal atmosphere.

Duo EnPassant brings the warmth of live violin and cello to wedding ceremonies and receptions. Based in Tallinn, Estonia, the duo consists of violinist Merily Leotoots and cellist Anna Ryland-Jones.

Both musicians are alumni of London's Guildhall School of Music & Drama. Their background in chamber music brings an attentive, acoustic approach to each celebration.

We perform throughout Estonia and consider selected weddings abroad. The programme and practical details are arranged individually.

Where we perform

Estonia and selected destinations abroad.

We are based in Tallinn and perform at weddings across Estonia. International celebrations can also be considered, with travel and logistical details included in a personalised quotation.

For a destination wedding, please share your location, date and approximate schedule so we can assess the practical arrangements.

Questions

A few practical details.

Can we choose our own music?

Yes. You can choose from our repertoire and ask about a specific song. If an arrangement is needed, we will discuss the options in advance.

Can you perform outside Tallinn?

Yes, throughout Estonia. Travel costs may apply outside Tallinn, depending on the location.

Do you perform at weddings abroad?

We consider selected international engagements. Please include the destination and date in your enquiry so that we can assess availability and travel arrangements.

How much does a performance cost?

Starting prices are available on our pricing page. The final quote depends on location, duration, date and repertoire.

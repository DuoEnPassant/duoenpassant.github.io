Biographies

Duo biography

Short biography

Duo EnPassant is a violin and cello chamber duo featuring Merily Leotoots and Anna Ryland-Jones. Both musicians are alumni of London’s Guildhall School of Music & Drama. Their artistic work centres on classical chamber music and concert performance, with appearances in Estonia, Finland and the United Kingdom.

Full biography

Duo EnPassant brings together violinist Merily Leotoots and cellist Anna Ryland-Jones. The ensemble is centred on classical chamber music for violin and cello, with concert performance at the core of its artistic work.

Before establishing Duo EnPassant, Merily and Anna performed together in chamber ensembles and at festivals across Estonia, Finland and the United Kingdom. Their first meeting took place in Estonia, where a natural musical connection developed through shared chamber-music projects.

Both musicians are alumni of London’s Guildhall School of Music & Drama, where they studied at different times. Duo EnPassant grew from their continuing collaboration and a shared aim to develop repertoire, concert programmes and a distinct musical identity as a violin and cello duo.
